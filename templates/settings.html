Step 1 — create your Dropbox app

  1. Open the Dropbox App Console and click Create app.
  2. Choose Scoped access, then App folder access, and give it any name (for example “MyLife backup”).
  3. Open the app's Permissions tab, tick files.content.write, files.content.read and files.metadata.read, then click Submit.
  4. On the Settings tab, copy the App key and App secret into the boxes below and click Save settings.

Step 2 — authorize

  1. Click here to authorize MyLife with Dropbox, approve access, then copy the code Dropbox shows you.
  2. Paste that code in the box below and click Save settings again.
